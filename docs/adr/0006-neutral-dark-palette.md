# Near-black neutral palette, warm accent

v2 drifted to a green, swampy look. The v1 palette (commit 26defa1) read better: near-black neutral surfaces, soft off-white text, and one warm yellow/amber accent, in the spirit of Monkeytype but not a copy. v3 goes back to that: neutral dark greys for every surface, green only for "ok" states, and the v1 accent for actions and highlights. Factory and town art keep their own colors but sit on the neutral frame.
