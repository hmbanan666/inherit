# Let the town wait

This decision supersedes ADR 0003. Players should be able to take their time learning the factory. Chapter one has no timers on requests or orders. Both wait and pay in full, and each story beat opens the next. Players can settle the debt whenever they are ready, in full or in optional installments without due days or interest. Town time has no gameplay meaning in chapter one. The clock stays internal and only changes the light on the town and factory. Showing the day or time of day again requires a story reason and a new ADR.
