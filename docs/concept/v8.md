# Inherit v8: quests at Kettlewick Works

Status: accepted 23 November 2026 ([ADR 0022](../adr/0022-quest-loop-no-loss.md), [ADR 0021](../adr/0021-v8-inherit-a-factory.md)).

The player inherits Uncle's workshop, Kettlewick Works, in a village. Villagers ask for things. The works do not play themselves.

## The loop

One villager, one quest, then the works wait. The player takes the quest. The card states the goal and why the current program is not enough. That why line is the hint: it may name a field, a brick, or a shop item, and it never shows the expression to paste.

Checks sit under the editor. They read the live world every tick. Delivery counts start at zero when the quest is taken, and a quest that needs a fresh baseline resets it then. The exact baselines are in the [first playable spec](../specs/v8-first-playable.md). They turn as the world runs. They never complete the quest and they never cost anything. When every check passes, **Complete quest** pays that quest's credits and XP. Shipping the goods does not pay. The next quest stays put until the player takes it, and only once the level is high enough.

Nothing repeats. After the last quest the card says more is coming.

Credits rise only from that button and fall only in the shop. They never go below zero. There is no rent, no bankruptcy, no deadline, and no pause. The clock keeps ticking, including while the tab is hidden. There is no speed button. The player's files, including `lib/`, are never replaced. **Start over** asks first, and it is the only reset.

Each quest's checks stay open under the program that cleared the previous quest. Later quests are easier if that code has been pulled into `lib/`.

## Shop

The shop is a tab on the quest card. A purchase applies at once and stays. Prices: Hotter furnace 60 (a batch finishes in 2 ticks), Bigger cart 40 (capacity +2), Bigger hopper 50 (12 ore and 6 coal), Second drone 120, CPU +10 for 80. Buying an item never turns a check green. `furnace.alloy()` and `prospect()` are not sold. Taking the quest that needs the function grants it, and the API panel shows it then, marked new while that quest is open. Calling one before that says which quest unlocks it. A save that already bought `alloy` or `prospect` keeps the function. Nothing is refunded.

Harrow is offered at level 4, before Tomas, and does not raise the level. The other six quests still cross one level each. Vein A and vein B are poor (5 ticks, 1 rock). Vein C, revealed by prospect(), is rich (1 tick, 2 rock). Veins do not run out.

Starting credits are 300.

## What is on screen

The arrangement is [the screen layout](../specs/v8-screen-layout.md). The charcoal frame stays: nav, quest card with the villager portrait, the works in the center, the file tree and editor on the right, checks and the console beside the code. Settings open API, Charts, and Start over. Deploy sits on the editor and shows when the code differs. The nav says Running, and shows credits, the level bar, and CPU used/limit.

The tick is 0.5 s. One drone starts at the depot. Vein A is poor ore and vein B is poor coal. Vein C is hidden until `prospect()`. The cart holds one kind, read as `drone.cargoKind`, including ingots and steel. `dump()` drops ore or coal. Mining a different rock dumps the old ore or coal and leaves an info line. Ingots and steel are not dumped. The depot accepts any amount of ore and coal, and it is where villagers take ingots and steel. The furnace hopper holds 6 ore and 3 coal; unload fills the free space and keeps the rest, and a full hopper names the drone and the cap. The furnace turns 2 ore into 1 ingot in 4 ticks, and `furnace.alloy()` turns 2 ore and 1 coal into 1 steel. Output holds 6 ingots and 6 steel. The CPU limit starts at 30. A fresh `main.ts` is one function and a comment. Uncle's loop, with `const home = false`, still fills the cart at vein A.

## Later

ADR 0022 decides these, and the code does not have them yet. Do not fill the gap with a deadline, rent, or a wipe.

- Speed bonus: +25% XP for finishing within a par time shown on the card, with no penalty.
- Shop tiers by level, beside the villager unlocks that already exist.
- Audit: a chapter summary after the chain. No loss.
- Next inheritance: a new site. The player's `lib/`, credits, and XP carry over.
