# Kettlewick Works through a newcomer's first handovers

24 November 2026 · Source audit of the fresh v8 game at [c5b96d2, after PR #90](https://github.com/hmbanan666/inherit/tree/c5b96d2c51bfce8fcc8a65213988215a61a248a7). Ines offers the player a Run button and asks for sixteen ingots. The starter can log `smeltOnce ok` while leaving the furnace cold. Even after a successful edit, one Run replaces one press of Smelt. The player still has to fetch 32 ore.

The walkthrough below is a source-based prediction. Times are illustrative, click counts state their assumptions, and the comments about distraction come from the owner. Source links use the audit revision unless marked **#91**. [PR #91](https://github.com/hmbanan666/inherit/pull/91) was already on `main` when the audit branch was created.
